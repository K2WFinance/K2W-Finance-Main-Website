Where do I begin if I have several goals?

Start with essential expenses and emergency needs. List your goals by date and importance. This helps you decide what needs to remain accessible and what can be set aside for longer.

How do fixed and recurring deposits differ?

A fixed deposit starts with a lump sum. A recurring deposit builds through regular contributions. Tenure, interest calculation, missed-payment rules and withdrawal conditions depend on the provider.

Are calculator results guaranteed?

No. Results use the amount, assumed rate and tenure you enter. They exclude taxes, charges and product-specific conditions. They are planning illustrations, not quotes or return guarantees.

What should I check about a co-operative deposit?

Confirm the exact legal entity, eligibility, applicable protections, audited information, repayment terms and early withdrawal rules. Do not assume a society deposit has the same protections as a bank deposit.

Can I plan for a regular monthly payout?

Some deposit structures offer periodic payouts. Available amounts, frequency, tenure and repayment conditions depend on the provider. Discuss these alongside your income needs and liquidity requirements.
